Ty's Guitar Shop Repairs

An Android application designed to manage guitar repair tickets, inventory parts, and repair reports.

Download Android APK

Requires Android 8.0 or higher

Application Features

Repair Ticket Management

Create, update, search, and delete guitar repair tickets.

Inventory Parts

Manage inventory parts associated with individual repair tickets.

Date Notifications

Schedule notifications for repair dates and inventory parts.

Reporting and Sharing

Generate repair ticket summary reports and share repair information.

Installation Instructions

  1. Download the APK using the button above.
  2. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device.
  3. If prompted, allow installation from this source in your Android settings.
  4. Follow the prompts to install the application.
  5. Launch TGS Repairs and create an employee account or log in.
